Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fable 5?

Fable 5 is Anthropic's most advanced frontier model. Focused on deep reasoning, long-running agentic work, and professional-level programming. Offers cutting-edge reasoning and extended thinking capabilities for complex problems.

What's the difference between Fable 5 and Opus 4.8?

Fable 5 is the next generation of reasoning, focused on autonomous agents and advanced programming. Opus 4.8 remains a robust general-purpose model. Fable 5 is better for tasks requiring deep analysis; Opus 4.8 is ideal for general conversation and fast processing.

What is the [1m] variant?

[1m] refers to Fable 5's 1 million token variant. It allows working with entire repositories, long conversations, and hundreds of files simultaneously. Ideal for complex code analysis and long-running agents.

What is extended thinking?

Extended thinking is a capability that allows the model to think deeply before answering. The model explores multiple paths, validates hypotheses, and refines its reasoning. Perfect for complex problems that require careful analysis.

How do I use Fable 5 in Claude Code?

Use the /model fable command to switch to Fable 5 in your conversation. Or use the open source stuntdouble skill for automatic selection. Fable 5 is available in Claude Code CLI, web, IDE, and desktop versions.

Is this page official from Anthropic?

No. This is an unofficial demo page built with the open source stuntdouble skill. It's a demonstration showcase. For official information, visit claude.com and api.anthropic.com.
